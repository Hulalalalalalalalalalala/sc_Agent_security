The vendored package verifies RFC 9449 proofs: typ, alg, JWS signature, htm, htu, iat, jti replay, nonce, and ath.
The experiment entry compares the resulting RFC 7638 thumbprint with the access token cnf.jkt claim.
ES256 only. Composite algorithms advertised by the package are not used.
